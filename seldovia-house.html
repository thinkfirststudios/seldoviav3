Seldovia House

Apartment-style senior and family housing in the heart of town.

A beautiful apartment-style multi-family and senior housing community right in the heart of Seldovia and Alaskan adventures.

Located at 350 Alder Street, Seldovia House offers one- and two-bedroom apartments with a community room, a shared greenhouse and gardens, and on-site laundry, all within a short walk of the beach, businesses, and medical services. Seldovia House is owned and managed by the Cook Inlet Housing Authority.

Income eligibility applies, with senior and family housing categories available. A deposit equal to one month's rent and a 12-month lease are required. Tenant pays electric. Pets allowed with approval and a pet deposit.
