Why Seldovia?

If you've been dreaming about a waterfront home, a quiet cabin tucked into the trees, a fishing retreat, or a beautiful piece of land with room to build, Seldovia offers opportunities that are becoming harder to find.

Here you'll discover rugged mountains, protected bays, abundant wildlife, world-class fishing, miles of shoreline, and an authentic Alaska lifestyle that moves at a different pace. Our community is accessible only by boat or plane, which has helped preserve the close-knit, welcoming character that we treasure.

People don't just come for the scenery, they come because they want a place where neighbors know one another, children still have room to explore, and life feels a little more connected.

I care deeply about this community because it's home. Every person I help has the potential to become not just another client, but a future neighbor and friend. That perspective guides everything I do. Whether you're buying your first property in Seldovia, looking for a vacation cabin, investing in waterfront land, or preparing to sell, I'd be honored to help you every step of the way.

Welcome to Seldovia. I look forward to helping you discover whether this little corner of Alaska is the right fit!
