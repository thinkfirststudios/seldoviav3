Seldovia Arts Council

Music, theater, film, and fine arts in our community by the sea.

Supporting cultural and educational activities in the arts and sciences by sponsoring and encouraging music, theater, film, fine arts, presentations, workshops, nature tours, and running and cycling races in our community by the sea.

The Seldovia Arts Council is a nonprofit organization under the auspices of the City of Seldovia. Its mission is to provide quality arts events and arts education through performances, workshops, and educational opportunities for our small town on Kachemak Bay.

Get involved

Become a member, sponsor, or donor to help keep the arts thriving in Seldovia. Performers can reach out through the council's "Interested in Performing?" form, and tickets and donations are handled through their Square site.
