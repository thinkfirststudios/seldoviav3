## Do you need a lawyer?

Honestly, for a community guide like this, plain policies like these are what most small sites launch with. The one area that's regulated is **real estate advertising**, so it's worth asking whether your brokerage has its own website rules, or having whoever handles your compliance glance at the "Real estate information" section and your license line.

Buy Me a Coffee keeps payments on their own site, so that doesn't change things. A closer legal look would matter more later only if the site started taking payments directly, added user accounts, or ran a contest with prizes.
