This Privacy Policy explains what information Seldovia.com collects, how we use it, and the outside services the site relies on. Seldovia.com is a community website for the town of Seldovia, Alaska, operated by Seldovia Property (Jenny Chissus, Broker/Owner). If you have any questions, contact us at [CONFIRM contact email — e.g. jenny@seldoviaproperty.com].

Information you give us

We only collect the information you choose to send through the forms on the site:

  • Contact form — your name, email address, the subject or topic you pick, and your message.
  • Add your household to the directory — your name, and any phone number, email, mailing address, or photo you choose to include. You decide, field by field, what is shown publicly and what stays private.
  • Add a business listing — the business name, owner name, category, phone, email, address, hours, and description you provide.
  • Photo submissions — any photos you upload and the name or caption you include with them.

Information collected automatically

Seldovia.com does not use cookies, advertising, analytics, or tracking pixels of its own. We do not build profiles of visitors. As with any website, the services that host the site and deliver its content (listed below) may record standard technical information such as your IP address and browser type in their own server logs.

How we use your information

  • To reply to messages and requests you send us.
  • To publish directory and business listings you submit — after review, and showing only the fields you marked public.
  • To display photos you submit to the site.
  • To operate and improve the community features of the site.

We do not sell your personal information.

Your privacy choices in the directory

When you add yourself or your household to the phone book, each detail (phone, email, address) has its own public or private setting. Anything left private is not displayed publicly. You can ask us to change or remove your listing at any time.

Outside services the site uses

Seldovia.com relies on the following third-party services. Each has its own privacy policy that governs how it handles data.

  • Supabase — stores form submissions and uploaded photos (database and file hosting).
  • Web3Forms — delivers contact-form messages to us by email [CONFIRM — only if enabled at launch; currently messages save to the site's own inbox].
  • Google Calendar — shows community events. Google may set cookies when the calendar is displayed.
  • Tockify — provides the community events schedule.
  • YouTube and Vimeo — play property videos where a listing includes one. These may set their own cookies when a video loads.
  • Open-Meteo, NOAA (Tides & Currents), and the National Weather Service — supply weather, tide, and marine conditions. No personal information is sent to them.
  • wsrv.nl (Weserv) — delivers the harbor webcam image.
  • Google Fonts and jsDelivr — deliver fonts and code the site uses; these may log your IP address as your browser loads them.
  • GitHub Pages — hosts the website [CONFIRM hosting provider at launch].

Children's privacy

This site is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has sent us information, contact us and we will remove it.

Keeping and removing your information

We keep submissions for as long as needed to run the directory and community features. To update or remove your information or listing, email us at [CONFIRM contact email].

Security

We take reasonable steps to protect the information the site collects. No method of storage or transmission over the internet is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will change the effective date at the top of this page.
